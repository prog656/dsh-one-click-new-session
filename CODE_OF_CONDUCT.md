# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity, disability, nationality, or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Respect maintainers' time — include reproduction steps when reporting a problem.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that violate these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders. Report problems via GitHub's "Report content" feature or by opening a minimal issue asking for a private contact.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
